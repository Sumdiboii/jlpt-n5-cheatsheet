**Grammar Concepts & Conjugation Rules (Lesson 20)**

**1. Polite Form vs. Plain/Casual Form (Introduction)**

* **Simple Explanation:** Up to Lesson 19, you have learned the **polite form** (ending in *desu* / *masu*), which is used with acquaintances, superiors, and in public. Lesson 20 introduces the **plain/casual form** (informal dictionary form / ta-form / nai-form / da-form), which is used when speaking with close friends, family members, or people of equal or younger status.

---

**2. Casual Conversation (Dropping Particles & Conversational Habits)**

* **Simple Explanation:** In casual speech, particles like **は** (*wa*), **が** (*ga*), **を** (*o*), and **に** (*ni*) are frequently omitted when the meaning is clear from context.
* **Example 1:**
* **Polite:** あした、どこへ行きますか。
* **Casual:** あした、どこ行く？ (*Ashita, doko iku?*)
* **Translation:** Where are you going tomorrow?



---

**3. Expressing Affirmation and Negation Casually (うん / ううん)**

* **Simple Explanation:** Instead of using **はい** (*hai*) and **いいえ** (*iie*), casual conversation uses **うん** (*un* for "yes") and **ううん** (*uun* for "no").
* **Example 1:**
* **Question:** もう昼ご飯を食べた？ (*Mou hiru-gohan o tabeta?*) $\rightarrow$ Did you already eat lunch?
* **Answer:** うん、食べた。 (*Un, tabeta.*) $\rightarrow$ Yeah, I did.



---

**4. Addressing People (Kun / Chan Suffixes)**

* **Simple Explanation:**
* **～くん (*-kun*)**: Used mainly for boys, male children, or male peers/colleagues of equal or lower status.
* **～ちゃん (*-chan*)**: Used affectionately for babies, young children, pets, or very close female friends/family members.



---

**5. Asking "How?" or "By what means?" (どうやって)**

* **Simple Explanation:** Use **どうやって** (*dou yatte*) to ask about the method or way something is done ("How do you do [verb]?").
* **Example:**
* **Kanji:** どうやって駅へ行きますか。
* **Without Kanji:** どうやってえきへいきますか。
* **Romaji:** Dou yatte eki e ikimasu ka.
* **Translation:** How do you get to the station?
